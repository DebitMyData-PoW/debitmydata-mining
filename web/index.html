        Where the figures come from

        Blocks. Who found each block comes from the chain: the text a pool writes in its blocks, or the address it pays, as DebitMyData's pool list knows them (the same list as the mempool explorer and DMD Stats). A miner the list doesn't know shows by the text it writes, or its address.

        A pool's hashrate, miners, fees and ports come from its own API, read every minute; a pool without a public API shows what it found on the chain, and what its operator published. The network's hashrate is worked out from the blocks: the work they took divided by the time they took.

        Luck compares the blocks a pool found with those its hashrate should have found at the difficulty of the time: above 100%, it found more. Over a day, luck swings a lot; over a week it says more.
